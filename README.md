## About

I'm a Computer Science student on the APU × De Montfort dual degree who builds software people actually use.

As a **Software Development Intern at Cloudify Asia**, I built **Spark CRM** from scratch and took it into production: frontend, backend, database, AI features, email integrations and deployment. The first customer purchase order arrived within **12 hours** of a Spark quotation, the fastest turnaround the company had seen.

Outside work I build tools I want to exist, like **[PromptForge](https://github.com/Maneet7805/PromptForge)**, a Windows tray app that rewrites rough prompts with an LLM on a hotkey. I care about the boring parts too: CI, tests, encrypted secrets, and READMEs someone else can follow.

**Open to:** software engineering internships · full-stack and AI product work · backend and platform roles
